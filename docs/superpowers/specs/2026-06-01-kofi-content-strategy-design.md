# Ko-fi Content Strategy — Santech Systems

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Scope:** Ko-fi page setup, membership tiers, profile copy, and first posts plan for Claritas / Santech Systems

---

## 1. Goals

- Enable tip-based and recurring support from Claritas users
- Position the account generically as Santech Systems (not Claritas-only) to leave room for future products
- Satisfy Stripe's content requirements for payment approval
- Keep maintenance low — content strategy is sustainable with infrequent posts

**Not in scope:** social media strategy, paid ads, SEO.

---

## 2. Approach

Phased rollout:

1. **Phase 1 — Launch:** Set up profile, tiers, Ko-fi Goal, publish launch post
2. **Phase 2 — Activation:** Dev update post + first member interactions
3. **Phase 3 — Organic growth:** Technical post to attract developer audience

---

## 3. Ko-fi Goal

- **Type:** $5/month recurring goal (or one-off donations)
- **Label:** "Keep the server lights on"
- Low bar intentionally — achievable early, creates momentum signal

---

## 4. Membership Tiers

| Tier | Name | Price | Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `GND` | $1/month | *Every circuit needs a ground. You're the foundation.* |
| 2 | `VCC` | $3/month | Name in release credits + early release notes |
| 3 | `Magic Smoke` | $7/month | Beta builds + priority feature vote |

**Tier naming rationale:** Electronics-themed, recognizable to the target audience (EE/hardware engineers), `Magic Smoke` references the informal term for burned components — memorable and funny to the audience.

---

## 5. Profile Description

> **Santech Systems** builds developer tools for engineers who hate wasting time.
>
> Our first app is **Claritas** — a Windows utility that captures schematics from Altium Designer and converts them to clean SVG files in one keystroke. No exports, no clicking through menus.
>
> Your support keeps the magic smoke inside the components — and the features shipping.

**Language:** English
**Tone:** Direct, technical, light humor

---

## 6. First Posts Plan

### Post 1 — Launch post *(publish on Ko-fi launch day)*
**Title:** "Claritas is now on Ko-fi! Here's what I'm building and why."
**Content:** Introduce the app, show a screenshot of the EMF→SVG workflow, link to download. CTA: join a tier or leave a tip.

### Post 2 — Dev update *(1–2 weeks after launch)*
**Title:** "What's next for Claritas — roadmap & feature requests"
**Content:** List planned features, invite `Magic Smoke` members to vote on priorities. Activates the premium tier's main benefit.

### Post 3 — Technical post *(2–3 weeks after launch)*
**Title:** "How I built a persistent Inkscape shell to convert schematics in < 1 second"
**Content:** Deep-dive on `inkscapeShell.js` — the persistent shell approach that eliminates cold-start latency. Targets developers and engineers, demonstrates technical depth, drives organic discovery.

---

## 7. Success Criteria

- Ko-fi page live with profile, tiers, and goal configured
- Stripe payment approval obtained
- 3 initial posts published within first month
- At least 1 member in any tier within 30 days
